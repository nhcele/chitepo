## Pipeline Overview

### Production Stages

1. **Pre-Production** (40% of time)
   - Script writing
   - Storyboarding
   - Equipment setup
   - Location/scene preparation

2. **Production** (20% of time)
   - Recording/filming
   - Capturing multiple takes
   - B-roll footage

3. **Post-Production** (35% of time)
   - Editing
   - Graphics and animations
   - Audio mixing
   - Color correction

4. **Quality Control** (5% of time)
   - Review and revision
   - Final approval

### Video Production Workflow

```
Script Writing → Storyboarding → Recording → Raw Footage Review 
→ Editing → Graphics/Audio → Review → Revisions → Final Approval 
→ Encoding → Upload → Distribution
```

## Pre-Production

### 1. Script Writing

**Requirements:**
- Write script based on lesson content (using text lesson as foundation)
- Script length: 10-20 minutes per video lesson
- Speaking rate: 150-160 words per minute
- Include pauses for emphasis and comprehension

**Script Template:**
```markdown
# Video Script: [Lesson Title]

## Metadata
- Course: [Course Name]
- Module: [Module Number]
- Lesson: [Lesson Number]
- Duration Target: [X] minutes
- Instructor: [Name]

## Introduction (30-60 seconds)
[Opening hook, learning objectives preview]

## Main Content (8-16 minutes)
[Section 1: Core Concepts]
[Section 2: Practical Applications]
[Section 3: Examples and Case Studies]

## Conclusion (30-60 seconds)
[Key takeaways, next steps]
```

**Script Guidelines:**
- Use conversational, accessible language
- Include visual cues: [SHOW: Diagram of DCC structure]
- Mark emphasis points: [EMPHASIZE: This is critical]
- Note slide transitions: [SLIDE: Title slide appears]
- Include time markers every 2-3 minutes

### 2. Storyboarding

**Purpose:** Visual planning of each video segment

**Storyboard Template:**
- Scene number
- Visual description
- Script excerpt
- Graphics/animations needed
- Duration estimate
- Notes

**Elements to Plan:**
- Instructor presence (talking head vs. screen recording)
- Screen shares for demonstrations
- Graphics and animations
- B-roll footage
- Text overlays and captions

### 3. Equipment Setup

**Required Equipment:**

**Basic Setup (Minimal):**
- HD webcam or DSLR camera (1080p minimum)
- External microphone (lapel or USB)
- Good lighting (ring light or softbox)
- Stable tripod or mount
- Clean, professional background

**Professional Setup (Recommended):**
- 4K camera (DSLR or mirrorless)
- Professional microphone (shotgun or lapel with interface)
- Three-point lighting kit
- Green screen or branded backdrop
- Teleprompter for script reading
- Multiple camera angles (if budget allows)

**Software:**
- Screen recording: OBS Studio, Camtasia, or ScreenFlow
- Video editing: Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve
- Graphics: Adobe After Effects, Canva Pro, or Keynote
- Teleprompter: Teleprompter Pro or CuePrompter

### 4. Location/Scene Preparation

**Studio Setup:**
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Consistent lighting throughout recording
- Minimal ambient noise
- Branded elements (logo, colors)
- Professional appearance

**On-Location Filming:**
- Scout locations in advance
- Check lighting conditions
- Test audio quality
- Plan for weather contingencies
- Obtain necessary permissions

## Production

### 1. Recording Process

**Best Practices:**
- Record in short segments (5-10 minutes each)
- Start each segment with slate (course, module, lesson, take number)
- Record multiple takes for difficult sections
- Keep energy levels consistent
- Maintain eye contact with camera
- Use natural gestures and expressions

**Recording Checklist:**
- [ ] Camera properly focused and framed
- [ ] Audio levels tested and set
- [ ] Lighting checked and adjusted
- [ ] Script and teleprompter ready
- [ ] Background and set clean
- [ ] Backup recording started (if possible)
- [ ] Clock/timer visible for duration tracking

### 2. Screen Recording (If Applicable)

**For Software Demonstrations:**
- Use clean desktop background
- Close unnecessary applications
- Set appropriate screen resolution (1920x1080)
- Record at 30fps minimum
- Include cursor highlight effects
- Use zoom/pan for detail

**For Presentations:**
- Use branded slide templates
- Ensure readable font sizes
- Include animation cues in script
- Record narration separately for flexibility

### 3. B-Roll Footage

**Types of B-Roll:**
- Location shots (relevant to content)
- People in action
- Close-ups of objects/documents
- Establishing shots
- Cutaway shots

**B-Roll Guidelines:**
- Record 3-5x more footage than needed
- Use slow, steady camera movements
- Hold shots for 5-10 seconds minimum
- Shoot from multiple angles
- Maintain visual consistency

## Post-Production

### 1. Editing Process

**Editing Workflow:**

**Step 1: Organization**
- Import all footage to editing software
- Organize by lesson/module
- Label files clearly
- Create project bins (A-roll, B-roll, graphics, audio)

**Step 2: Rough Cut**
- Assemble clips in order
- Remove obvious mistakes
- Trim long pauses
- Add basic transitions

**Step 3: Fine Cut**
- Tighten timing and pacing
- Add B-roll strategically
- Include graphics and animations
- Refine audio levels

**Step 4: Polish**
- Color correction
- Audio mixing and enhancement
- Text overlays and captions
- Final transitions and effects

### 2. Graphics and Animations

**Required Graphics:**
- Title slide with course/lesson name
- Learning objectives overlay
- Key concept callouts
- Section dividers
- Lower thirds for instructor names
- Closing slide with next lesson preview

**Animation Guidelines:**
- Keep animations subtle and professional
- Use consistent motion graphics style
- Ensure text remains readable
- Time animations to speech
- Include zoom effects for emphasis

**Brand Elements:**
- Use official Chitepo School colors
- Include logo appropriately
- Maintain consistent typography
- Follow brand guidelines

### 3. Audio Enhancement

**Audio Processing:**
- Remove background noise
- Normalize audio levels
- Add subtle compression
- Enhance voice clarity
- Balance music (if used) to 10-15% of voice level

**Music Guidelines:**
- Use royalty-free music
- Choose instrumental tracks
- Match mood to content
- Keep volume low
- Fade in/out smoothly

### 4. Captions and Subtitles

**Requirements:**
- Generate accurate captions for all videos
- Sync timing precisely
- Include speaker identification
- Mark non-speech sounds [music], [applause]
- Format for readability

**Accessibility:**
- Ensure 99%+ accuracy
- Review for correct terminology
- Check spelling of proper nouns
- Include multiple language options (if available)

## Quality Control

### 1. Technical Quality Checklist

**Video Quality:**
- [ ] Resolution: 1080p minimum (4K preferred)
- [ ] Frame rate: Consistent 30fps or 60fps
- [ ] Color: Proper white balance and exposure
- [ ] Focus: Sharp throughout
- [ ] No visible compression artifacts

**Audio Quality:**
- [ ] Clear, intelligible speech
- [ ] Consistent volume levels
- [ ] No background noise or echo
- [ ] Proper balance between voice and music
- [ ] Professional sound quality

**Content Quality:**
- [ ] Accurate information
- [ ] Clear explanations
- [ ] Appropriate pacing
- [ ] Engaging presentation
- [ ] Aligned with learning objectives

### 2. Review Process

**Review Stages:**

1. **Self-Review** (Instructor)
   - Check for accuracy
   - Identify obvious errors
   - Note areas needing improvement

2. **Peer Review** (Content Team)
   - Verify factual accuracy
   - Check alignment with curriculum
   - Provide feedback on clarity

3. **Final Review** (Academic Lead)
   - Overall quality assessment
   - Brand consistency check
   - Final approval or revision requests

### 3. Revision Process

**Revision Guidelines:**
- Track all feedback systematically
- Prioritize critical errors first
- Maintain version control
- Document all changes
- Re-review revised sections

## Distribution & Storage

### 1. Video Encoding

**Output Formats:**

**Primary Format (Platform):**
- Codec: H.264 or H.265
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Audio: AAC, 192 kbps, stereo

**Archive Format (Storage):**
- Codec: ProRes or DNxHD (high quality)
- Resolution: Native recording resolution
- Maintain original quality for future use

### 2. Storage Organization

**File Naming Convention:**
```
[CourseID]_[ModuleNum]_[LessonNum]_[Version]_[Date].mp4
Example: DCC01_M01_L02_v01_20251127.mp4
```

**Directory Structure:**
```
videos/
├── raw_footage/
│   ├── course_17/
│   ├── course_18/
│   └── course_19/
├── edited/
│   ├── course_17/
│   ├── course_18/
│   └── course_19/
├── final/
│   ├── course_17/
│   ├── course_18/
│   └── course_19/
└── archive/
```

### 3. Video Hosting

**Platform Requirements:**
- Reliable CDN for global delivery
- Adaptive bitrate streaming
- Caption support
- Analytics and tracking
- Mobile optimization
- Playback speed controls

**Recommended Platforms:**
- Vimeo Pro
- YouTube (unlisted)
- Custom CDN (CloudFront, Cloudflare)
- Learning Management System integration

### 4. Delivery Checklist

**Before Publishing:**
- [ ] Final video file encoded correctly
- [ ] Captions/subtitles uploaded and synced
- [ ] Thumbnail image created
- [ ] Metadata (title, description, tags) added
- [ ] Analytics tracking enabled
- [ ] Tested on multiple devices/browsers
- [ ] Links verified and working

## Technical Specifications

### Recording Specifications

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Color space: Rec. 709
- Audio: 48kHz, 16-bit minimum

**Recommended (Professional):**
- Resolution: 3840x2160 (4K UHD)
- Frame rate: 30fps or 60fps
- Color space: Rec. 2020 or DCI-P3
- Audio: 48kHz, 24-bit
- Bit depth: 10-bit color

### Delivery Specifications

**Platform Delivery:**
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps (variable)
- Audio: AAC, 192 kbps
- File size: ~500MB - 1.5GB per 20-minute video

**Archive Storage:**
- Format: MOV or MXF (ProRes/DNxHD)
- Resolution: Native recording resolution
- Uncompressed or lossless compression
- Maintains maximum quality for future use

## Production Schedule

### Timeline Per Video Lesson

**Typical Timeline (10-15 minute video):**

- **Pre-Production:** 8-12 hours
  - Script writing: 4-6 hours
  - Storyboarding: 2-3 hours
  - Setup and preparation: 2-3 hours

- **Production:** 2-4 hours
  - Recording: 1-2 hours
  - Multiple takes: 1-2 hours

- **Post-Production:** 10-15 hours
  - Editing: 6-8 hours
  - Graphics/animations: 2-3 hours
  - Audio enhancement: 1-2 hours
  - Captions: 1-2 hours

- **Quality Control:** 2-3 hours
  - Review: 1-2 hours
  - Revisions: 1-2 hours

**Total: 22-34 hours per video lesson**

### Batch Production

**Efficient Workflow:**
- Record multiple lessons in one session
- Batch similar post-production tasks
- Create reusable graphics templates
- Establish efficient review cycles

**Optimized Timeline (10 videos):**
- **Week 1-2:** Script writing (all videos)
- **Week 3:** Storyboarding and prep
- **Week 4:** Recording sessions (all videos)
- **Week 5-7:** Post-production (staggered)
- **Week 8:** Quality control and final delivery

## Best Practices Summary

1. **Plan thoroughly** - Pre-production is key to efficiency
2. **Record in segments** - Easier to edit and retake
3. **Maintain consistency** - Visual style, audio quality, branding
4. **Quality over speed** - Don't rush at the expense of quality
5. **Test everything** - Review on multiple devices/platforms
6. **Document processes** - Create templates and checklists
7. **Iterate and improve** - Refine pipeline based on feedback
