# Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology
## Platform Pitch for ZANU PF Leadership

---

## The Vision

**"Liberating the Mind, the Spirit, and the Nation"**

A comprehensive digital platform that brings the Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology to every party member, government official, and Zimbabwean worldwide—delivering systematic ideological education, practical governance training, and diaspora mobilization at unprecedented scale.

---

## The Challenge We Solve

### 1. **Inconsistent Political Education**
- Party members receive fragmented training across provinces
- No standardized curriculum ensuring ideological alignment
- Difficult to track who has been trained and to what level

### 2. **Mandatory Training Compliance**
- 2016 ZANU-PF resolution: "No representation without certification"
- Manual tracking of MPs, councillors, and officials is inefficient
- Grace periods and deadlines are hard to monitor and enforce

### 3. **Diaspora Disconnection**
- 31,200+ diaspora members across 9 global regions lack systematic engagement
- Virtual training is ad-hoc, not structured or certified
- Investment and voter mobilization potential remains untapped

### 4. **Capacity Gaps in Government**
- New councillors, mayors, and DCC members need rapid onboarding
- Vision 2030 implementation requires aligned, skilled officials
- Traditional leaders and judicial officers need specialized training

## Certification Pathways: Progressive Recognition

### **5 Levels of Achievement**

**Level 0: Orientation Certificate** (2 weeks, Free)
- Basic ideological awareness for all party members

**Level 1: Certificate in Political Ideology** (8-12 weeks)
- Foundation qualification for active members
- **Required for local government candidates**

**Level 2: Advanced Certificate** (16-24 weeks)
- Intermediate competence in ideology and governance
- **Required for parliamentary candidates**

**Level 3: Diploma in Political Ideology** (9-12 months)
- Advanced expertise, nationally recognized
- **Required for senate candidates and senior officials**

**Level 4: Master Trainer Certification** (6 weeks)
- Authorized to train party members at ward level
- Employment as official instructor

## Specialized Training Tracks

### **1. Government Officials Track** (Mandatory)

**Target Participants:**
- District Coordinating Committee (DCC) Members
- Mayors, Councillors, Municipal Directors
- Rural District Council Officials
- Traditional Leaders (Chiefs and Headmen)
- Judicial Officers

**Mandatory Requirements:**
- **New councillors:** Complete within 6 months of election
- **DCC members:** Complete within 1 year of appointment
- **Parliamentary candidates:** Level 2 minimum before nomination
- **Senate candidates:** Level 3 minimum before nomination

**Compliance Tracking:**
- Automated alerts at 30 days and 7 days before deadline
- Grace period of 6 months for extenuating circumstances
- Real-time dashboard showing compliance status by province, district, ward
- Administrator oversight for party leadership to monitor progress

**Funding:** Fully sponsored by government—no cost to officials
