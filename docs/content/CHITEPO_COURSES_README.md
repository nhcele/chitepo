# Chitepo School of Ideology - Course Catalog

## Overview

The Chitepo School of Ideology is a comprehensive political education and ideological training platform integrated into the Mindelta learning management system. Named after Herbert Chitepo, a prominent Zimbabwean nationalist and revolutionary, the school offers courses in political theory, African history, governance, and social transformation.

## Course Catalog

### 1. Pan-Africanism and African Unity
**Duration:** 7 hours | **Difficulty:** Intermediate

Explore the history, philosophy, and contemporary relevance of Pan-Africanism. This comprehensive course examines the movement for African unity, key historical figures, the role of the African Union, and strategies for economic and political integration across the continent.

**Modules:**
- Historical Foundations of Pan-Africanism
- Liberation Struggles and Independence
- Contemporary Pan-Africanism

**Key Topics:**
- Early Pan-African movements and key figures (Nkrumah, Nyerere, Cabral)
- The Manchester Conference 1945
- Decolonization and liberation struggles
- From OAU to African Union
- Agenda 2063: The Africa We Want
