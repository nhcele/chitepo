# Certification Pathways - Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology

## Overview

The Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology offers multiple certification pathways designed to meet the diverse needs of learners, from general ideological education to specialized training for government officials, diaspora members, and aspiring leaders. Each pathway is structured to provide progressive learning and credentialing that aligns with career and service objectives.

## Certification Framework

### Qualification Levels

```
Level 5: Honorary Doctorate → Recognition for exceptional contribution
         ↑
Level 4: Master Trainer → Qualified to train others
         ↑
Level 3: Diploma → Advanced expertise and leadership readiness
         ↑
Level 2: Advanced Certificate → Intermediate knowledge and skills
         ↑
Level 1: Certificate → Foundational understanding
         ↑
Level 0: Orientation → Basic awareness
```

## Pathway 1: General Ideological Education

**Target Audience:** Party members, general public, youth, interested citizens

### Level 0: Orientation Certificate
**Duration:** 2 Weeks | **Mode:** Online or Face-to-Face

**Requirements:**
- Complete 10-hour orientation program
- Pass basic quiz (60% minimum)
- Attend opening and closing sessions

**Curriculum:**
- Introduction to Herbert Chitepo and His Legacy
- Overview of Liberation History
- Party Ideology and Principles
- National Values and Patriotism
- Zimbabwe's Development Vision

**Certificate Type:** Participation Certificate

**Benefits:**
- Basic understanding of ideology
- Foundation for further studies
- Party membership recognition

---

### Level 1: Certificate in Political Ideology
**Duration:** 8-12 Weeks | **Mode:** Flexible

**Requirements:**
- Complete 4 core courses (minimum)
- 50% pass mark on all assessments
- 80% attendance (if face-to-face)
- Complete capstone essay (2,000 words)

**Core Courses (Choose 4 from 8):**
1. Pan-Africanism and African Unity
2. Revolutionary Theory and Practice
3. Political Economy and Development
4. Leadership and Governance
5. African History and Liberation Heritage
6. Social Transformation and Nation Building
7. International Relations and Diplomacy
8. Political Philosophy and Ideology

**Assessment:**
- Module quizzes: 30%
- Assignments: 20%
- Final exam: 30%
- Capstone essay: 20%

**Certificate Type:** Academic Certificate

**Benefits:**
- Comprehensive ideological foundation
- Eligibility for party positions
- Credit toward higher certifications
- Enhanced career opportunities

## Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

### Eligibility
- Relevant work experience (5+ years)
- Previous academic qualifications
- Demonstrated competencies
- Party leadership roles

### Process
1. Submit RPL application with portfolio
2. Assessment by RPL panel
3. Practical demonstration/interview
4. Credit awarded (up to 50% of program)

### Benefits
- Accelerated completion
- Recognition of experience
- Reduced costs
- Fast-track to higher levels
