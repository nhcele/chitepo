# Diaspora Engagement Program - Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology

## Overview

The Diaspora Engagement Program is a specialized virtual training initiative designed to maintain ideological connection, foster political participation, and facilitate economic contribution of Zimbabwean diaspora members to national development. The program leverages digital technology to overcome geographical barriers while strengthening ties between diaspora communities and the homeland.

## Vision Statement

**"Connecting Hearts, Minds, and Resources Across Borders for Zimbabwe's Development"**

To create an ideologically aligned, patriotic, and economically engaged diaspora community that actively contributes to Zimbabwe's transformation while maintaining strong cultural and political ties to the homeland.
