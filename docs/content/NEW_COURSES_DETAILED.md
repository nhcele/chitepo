### Course 17: District Coordinating Committee (DCC) Training
**Duration:** 7 hours | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Category:** Practical Governance

#### Course Description
Comprehensive training program for District Coordinating Committee members focusing on political mobilization, grassroots organizing, party-government coordination, and achieving electoral targets. This course equips DCC members with the skills necessary to effectively organize and mobilize communities while coordinating with local government structures.

#### Target Audience
- DCC Members and Officials
- District Secretaries
- Provincial Coordinators
- Ward Coordinators
- Cell Leaders

#### Learning Objectives
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:
- Organize and manage effective grassroots mobilization campaigns
- Coordinate party structures with local government agencies
- Develop and implement ward-based organizing strategies

- Build and maintain cell structures
- Facilitate community development initiatives
- Monitor and report on district political activities

## Implementation Notes

### Course Content Format
All courses include:
- **Video Lectures**: Professional production with subtitles
- **Text Lessons**: Downloadable PDFs with reading materials
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Knowledge checks after each module
- **Assignments**: Practical application exercises
- **Discussion Forums**: Peer and instructor interaction
- **Case Studies**: Real-world examples from Zimbabwe
- **Resource Libraries**: Additional readings and materials

### Instructor Team
Each course taught by:
- **Lead Instructor**: Subject matter expert
- **Guest Lecturers**: Practitioners and government officials
- **Teaching Assistants**: Support and facilitation
- **Mentors**: One-on-one guidance

### Assessment Structure
- **Module Quizzes**: 10% each (30% total)
- **Assignments**: 20%
- **Final Exam**: 30%
- **Practical Project**: 20%

### Certificate Requirements
- **Pass Mark**: 50%
- **Attendance**: 80% (for live sessions)
- **Project Completion**: Mandatory
- **No Outstanding Assignments**: All work submitted
