# Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology - Complete Platform Guide

## Executive Summary

The Herbert Chitepo School of Ideology is a comprehensive political education and ideological training platform integrated into the Mindelta LMS. Named after liberation hero Herbert Chitepo, the school offers a complete curriculum of **26 courses across 174 hours** organized into four main categories:

1. **Core Ideological Courses** (8 courses) - Foundation in Pan-Africanism, revolutionary theory, and political philosophy
2. **Contemporary Studies** (8 courses) - Modern issues including democracy, gender, environment, and human rights
3. **Practical Governance Track** (6 courses) - Specialized training for government officials and party structures
4. **Diaspora Engagement Program** (4 courses) - Virtual training for Zimbabweans living abroad

## Platform Overview

### Mission Statement
**"Liberating the Mind, the Spirit, and the Nation"**

To provide comprehensive ideological education, practical governance training, and political leadership development that aligns with Zimbabwe's liberation heritage and national development goals.

## Assessment and Certification

### Assessment Structure

**Continuous Assessment (40-50%):**
- Module quizzes: 15-20%
- Assignments and essays: 10-20%
- Discussion participation: 10-15%
- Field projects: 5-10%

**Final Assessment (50-60%):**
- Written examination: 30-40%
- Practical assessment: 15-20%
- Capstone project/thesis: 20-30%
- Oral presentation: 5%

### Pass Requirements
- Minimum pass mark: 50% (Certificate), 60% (Advanced), 70% (Diploma)
- Attendance: 90% residential, 80% virtual
- All projects completed
- No disciplinary issues
- Financial obligations cleared

### Graduation

**Quarterly Ceremonies:**
- March, June, September, December
- Harare (main), Provincial centers
- Special ceremonies for government officials
- Virtual participation for diaspora

**Graduation Package:**
- Official certificate (printed + digital)
- Blockchain-verified credential
- Graduation gown rental
- Professional photography
- Alumni membership
- Transcript of records

### Continuing Professional Development (CPD)

**Annual Requirements:**
- All certified members: 20 CPD points
- Master trainers: 40 CPD points
- Government officials: 30 CPD points

**CPD Activities:**
- Refresher courses (10 points)
- Conferences/workshops (5 points per day)
- Published articles (15 points)
