### 2. Government Officials Track Program

**Complete Document:** `GOVERNMENT_OFFICIALS_TRACK.md`

**Key Features:**
- Three-phase program structure (Orientation, Specialized Training, Practical Application)
- Five specialized tracks for different official categories
- Mandatory training for electoral candidates
- Fully government-sponsored for officials
- Train-the-trainer certification pathway

**Target Participants:**
1. District Coordinating Committee (DCC) Members
2. Local Government Officials (mayors, councillors)
3. Rural District Council Officials
4. Traditional Leaders (chiefs, headmen)
5. Judicial Officers (judges, magistrates)

**Program Duration:**
- Phase 1 (Orientation): 2 weeks
- Phase 2 (Specialized): 6-12 weeks (track dependent)
- Phase 3 (Practical): 4 weeks
- Total: 12-18 weeks for full program

**Certification Levels:**
- Level 1: Certificate of Completion (6-8 weeks)
- Level 2: Advanced Certificate (10-12 weeks)
- Level 3: Diploma in Governance and Ideology (3-6 months)
- Level 4: Train-the-Trainer Certification (Additional 4 weeks)

**Mandatory Requirements:**
- Parliamentary candidates: Minimum Level 2
- Local government candidates: Minimum Level 1
- Senate candidates: Minimum Level 3
- Mayoral candidates: Level 2 or above

**Annual Training Calendar:**
- Q1: New councillors, DCC members
- Q2: RDC officials (Provinces 1-5), Traditional leaders
- Q3: RDC officials (Provinces 6-10), Mayoral leadership
- Q4: Pre-election training, Train-the-trainer

**Success Targets:**
- 10,000 officials trained annually
- 85% completion rate
- 90% certification rate
- 5 million voter registration by 2028
- 500 community projects annually
